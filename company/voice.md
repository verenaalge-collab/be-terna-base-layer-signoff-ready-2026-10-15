# Voice

> SIGN-OFF: Approve the guidance, or add language-specific exceptions under
> Locale notes. One decision below.

## Tone
Clear, competent and collaborative B2B. Industry-aware and platform-specific when relevant.

Write from the buyer's process and desired operational improvement toward the
solution. Use direct sentences, concrete nouns and restrained claims. Sound
experienced and helpful, not promotional or abstract.

## Prefer
- Process outcomes and time-to-value
- Named platforms and solution families
- Customer collaboration language such as “side by side”
- Specific process language: finance, production, supply chain, sales, service, data governance and workflow automation
- Clear distinctions between BE-terna services, BE-terna intellectual property and vendor products

## Avoid
- Unsourced headcount, customer or project numbers
- Inflated partner tiers or awards
- Mixing Telefónica Tech cybersecurity claims into BE-terna offerings without approved wording
- Treating office location as a hard sales limit
- Unsupported superlatives and vague transformation language without a concrete process or solution
- Guaranteed outcomes, universal availability or implied compliance without evidence

## Locale notes
Keep country-only offerings, including Payroll Swiss, within their approved market and language scope.

- Translate meaning and buyer terminology; do not copy English phrasing mechanically.
- Confirm local product names, legal terminology and formality with each locale owner.
