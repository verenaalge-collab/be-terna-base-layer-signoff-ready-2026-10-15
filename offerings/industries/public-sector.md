## One-liner

Simplify administrative processes, relieve staff and improve digital citizen services with connected automation, data and case-management solutions.

## Who the buyer is

Public administrations at federal, state, cantonal and municipal level that want to develop existing processes and specialist systems (Fachverfahren) step by step rather than replace them.

## Typical challenges we solve

- Limited staff capacity tied up in standardized, repetitive work
- Media breaks between applications, mailboxes, documents and specialist systems
- Rising expectations for digital, always-available citizen and business services
- Data that exists but is not usable for steering, case handling and decisions

## Preferred offerings / platforms

- Automation: RPA, intelligent document processing and AI on UiPath and Microsoft Power Platform
- Data & BI: data integration and platforms such as Microsoft Fabric (OneLake, Fabric Warehouse) or Qlik-based data warehouses; visualization with Qlik or Power BI
- CRM / digital case management: Microsoft Dynamics 365 Customer Service, Contact Center, Power Platform and Power Pages
- AI as assistance only: decisions remain with people (stated on the page)

## Proof to prefer

- CS-007 Migrationsamt St. Gallen: automated suitable standard cases for renewing settlement permits, on UiPath (P-018)
- CS-008 Kanton Schwyz: automated a central payment process with Microsoft Power Automate (P-019)

## Markets where this industry focus is strong

- Switzerland is evidenced by both references; the page addresses federal, state, cantonal and municipal levels (D3).
