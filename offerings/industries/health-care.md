## One-liner

Connect hospital information systems, ERP, document management, portals and communication channels so clinics reduce manual work, use their data and steer patient and referrer communication.

## Who the buyer is

Hospitals, rehabilitation and specialist clinics, and outpatient care centres whose processes run across a hospital information system (KIS), ERP, DMS, portals, insurers and payers.

## Typical challenges we solve

- Manual steps between systems (Excel lists, e-mail, re-keying)
- A fragmented data basis for cases, revenue, costs, utilization, quality and resources
- Slow reporting and inconsistent KPIs for management, controlling and quality management
- High contact volumes in patient service
- Data protection, auditability and regulatory requirements for health data
- Scarce specialist and IT resources

## Preferred offerings / platforms

- Data & BI: healthcare data platform, management cockpits, quality and regulatory reporting, capacity and resource forecasting
- Automation: patient admission and master-data checks, insurer and payer matching, document checking and routing, discharge and administrative workflows with auditable execution
- CRM / customer engagement: patient access, contact center and omnichannel, referrer communication, follow-up communication
- Platforms named on the page: Microsoft Dynamics 365, Microsoft Power Platform, Microsoft Fabric, Qlik, UiPath
- BE-terna does **not** replace the hospital information system; it connects existing systems (stated on the page)
